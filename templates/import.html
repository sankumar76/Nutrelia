Add foods from MyFitnessPal

  1. On myfitnesspal.com open Reports → Printable Diary and pick the dates.
  2. Print the page and choose Save as PDF.
  3. Choose the PDF here. You can pick several.

Only new foods are added to your library. Your Nutrelia log isn't changed, and foods you already have aren't duplicated.
